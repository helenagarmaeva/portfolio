TPK COSMOSPRAY

Product Design / B2B / E-commerce / Design System

About the company

TPK COSMOSPRAY is a B2B supplier of perfume and cosmetics packaging: bottles, caps, pumps and components.

The task

Update the website and simplify the catalog experience: finding products, choosing quantities and placing wholesale orders.

My role

UX/UI design, user journey mapping, UI kit and implementation of interface solutions in Tilda.

User scenario

I mapped the main product search and ordering scenarios: searching by name or SKU, and browsing categories and filters. The User Flow covers variant selection, quantity, wholesale price tiers and order review before submitting a request.

Redesign

The homepage introduces the company through its offer, product categories, popular items and contact details. In the catalog, categories and product attributes sit in a separate column, helping buyers narrow their selection and compare products.

Homepage

Catalog

Screens

I demonstrated the main catalog scenarios: searching and filtering, choosing a product, changing its quantity and adding it to the cart. The price is recalculated automatically based on order volume.

Responsive design

Adapted the homepage, catalog, product details and contacts for mobile devices. A clear hierarchy keeps the key actions accessible: finding components, choosing a product and contacting the company.

UI kit

I brought together logo variants, a neutral palette with a purple accent, Inter Tight typography, and button and link states. The UI kit keeps new pages consistent and clearly distinguishes content from interactive elements.

Result

The redesign is live on the existing website. I brought the corporate pages, catalog and wholesale ordering journey into one consistent system, from product search to price calculation and submitting an order request. The project continues to evolve: new product journeys and catalog features are being added on top of the system.
