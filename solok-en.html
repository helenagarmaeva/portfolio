Solok Cosmetics

Web / B2C / E-com / Graphic design

About the brand

Solok Cosmetics is a manufacturer of natural skincare products. Quality, sustainability and product safety are the company’s core values.

The task

Develop a visual identity and an online store. Make discovering the brand, choosing products and purchasing cosmetics clear and convenient.

My role

UX research, UX/UI design, visual identity, packaging and label design.

Research

The project involved in-depth interviews with the target audience, as well as market research and an analysis of the competitors whose products the interviewees preferred.

I interviewed people of different ages about their use of skincare products.

Screens

I designed a scrub builder based on selected ingredients and a skin type quiz. Product pages include dedicated sections for descriptions, ingredients and reviews.

Mobile version

I paid particular attention to the mobile experience because the project has many complex features. Customers often shop for cosmetics on their phones, so the responsive layouts needed to look good and make every feature comfortable to use on a small screen.

Branding

Alongside the website, I designed boxes and product labels for the brand.
