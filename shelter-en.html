Dubovaya Roshcha

Illustration / Graphic Design

About the shelter

Dubovaya Roshcha is a municipal shelter home to more than 800 dogs. Many have been waiting for a family for years, so the shelter regularly takes part in adoption events and charity projects that help animals find a home.

My role

I developed the concept, illustrations and layout for a 2025 charity calendar, as well as a series of adoption cards for the shelter’s dogs. These volunteer projects were created to support the shelter and help the dogs find families.

2025 calendar

I created a series of 12 original illustrations featuring the shelter’s dogs — one for each month. The calendar had a print run of 150 copies and sold out before New Year. All proceeds were donated to the shelter.

01/12

Dog adoption cards

I designed individual adoption cards for dogs taking part in the Dom Foundation’s adoption event. Each card includes a photo, a short description of the dog’s personality, a volunteer’s contact details and a QR code, helping visitors get to know the dogs and easily contact their carers.
